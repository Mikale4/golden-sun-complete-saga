Tiles (keys)

Edits

Left-drag paints. Right-click picks the tile under the cursor. Middle-drag or Space+drag pans; wheel zooms.
The rip is the reference picture drawn under the tiles (slider sets its strength). "Passable view" shades every tile the party cannot walk: mountains, water, the edge.
Save writes js/maps/<map>_edits.js; the game applies it over the generated layout, so regenerating the layout never loses these.
