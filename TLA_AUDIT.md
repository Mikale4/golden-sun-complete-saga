# The Lost Age - completeness audit

Every section of `reference game mechanics/txt/guide and walkthrough gs2.txt` (walkthrough part, by heading and
line) against the game: the map files, the story scripts (`js/game/story_tla.js`, the maps' own scenes), the
runner plan (`runner/plan_tla.js`). Status: **built** = the area exists as a real map; **verified** = the runner
has played it end to end; **audited** = every event, chest, Djinni, hidden item, side quest and NPC scene the guide
names has been checked against the code; **stub** = placeholder map in tla_towns.js / tla_dungeons.js.

Legend for the per-section checklists: `[x]` present and checked, `[ ]` missing or unchecked, `[~]` partial.
